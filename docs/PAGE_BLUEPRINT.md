# Non-removable page blueprint

The OpenArt Seedance 2.5 page is the required content-framework reference. UI, motion, visual hierarchy, and interaction may change, but the following chapters must remain in this order unless the project owner explicitly changes the rule:

1. Global navigation
2. Hero: title, summary, generator, output video
3. Work Showcase — the Community-equivalent chapter, reframed as curated Seedance 2.5 scenarios because Wizstar does not currently have a community surface
4. Product advantages — five feature chapters
5. Getting started — three steps
6. Workflow scenarios — four use-case/workflow chapters
7. Direction tips — four tips
8. Editorial resources — three article slots
9. Sourced creator feedback
10. Product questions and answers
11. Closing action
12. Global footer

## Content rule

- A chapter may use temporary scaffolding while product content is pending.
- A chapter may not be removed, silently merged into another chapter, or replaced with a different information category.
- Unverified Wizstar claims must be labelled as pending rather than invented.
- Future design iterations modify presentation only; this document is the structural acceptance checklist.
