# Product facts · verified 2026-08-25

Source: signed-in Wizstar product interface and the internal project overview supplied by the owner.

## AI Video Generator

- Modes: Reference to Video, Keyframe to Video, Text to Video.
- Reference accepts image, video, and audio, up to 50 multimodal assets.
- Reference/Text durations: 4, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 seconds.
- Reference/Text resolutions: 480P and 720P.
- Reference/Text ratios: 9:16 and 16:9; output count: 1–4.
- Keyframe requires a first frame; end frame is optional.
- Keyframe prompt limit shown: 2,000 characters.
- Keyframe has no independent ratio picker in the inspected UI.

## Agent workflows

- E-commerce accepts product URL, product images, and selling points.
- Creative accepts text, images, or video and supports style/type choices.
- Both currently allow Seedance 2.5 selection.
- “Up to 300 sec”, “10+ languages”, and “up to 4K” describe Agent workflows, not one Seedance generation.

## Do not claim without new verification

- Dedicated Seedance 2.5 API endpoint
- Separate native-audio toggle
- Region editing in the three inspected modes
- 4K for one Seedance 2.5 generation
- Fixed credit price
